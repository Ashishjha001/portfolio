  <p><strong>Zero-click is not a verdict on content; it is a change in what content must do.</strong> Fund content in three roles: click-worthy, citable, and convertible. Decide the budget per role, and collect dated evidence before declaring any trend at your category.</p>
</div>

"Searches end without clicks" is now a common observation, and "SEO is dead" is the usual overreaction. Both the observation and the overreaction skip the decision that matters: which jobs your content does when the click is no longer guaranteed.

## The three content roles

| Role         | Job                                      | Success signal                                                     | Funding logic                   |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Click-worthy | Earn the visit for decision-stage intent | CTR on commercial queries                                          | Fund where clicks convert       |
| Citable      | Be the source AI answers quote           | Dated [baseline mentions](/insights/build-ai-visibility-baseline/) | Fund for presence, judge slowly |
| Convertible  | Convert owned traffic (email, product)   | Owned-channel engagement                                           | Fund independent of search      |

Most content budgets fail by funding only the first role and measuring it with second-role expectations.

## The evidence to collect before deciding

- Your own CTR by query class, from Search Console, over a defined window.
- A dated answer-visibility [baseline](/insights/build-ai-visibility-baseline/) for category prompts.
- CRM-side demand: branded search volume, direct visits, newsletter growth.
- Competitor presence in answers, sampled the same way.

Trends declared without a window, a sample and a source are anecdotes. The [methodology](/methodology/) defines the observation rules.

## Claims to avoid

- "X% of searches are zero-click" applied to your category without your data.
- "AI Overviews killed our traffic" before checking [access verification](/insights/technical-access-verification-checklist/) and window effects.
- Any plan that stops all content because clicks fell, while answer mentions rose.

## The decision, in one line

For each content cluster, choose a role, set a role-appropriate signal, and fund it quarterly against that signal. Review when the evidence changes, not when the discourse does.

For the measurement layer that separates roles cleanly, review the [measurement foundation](/services/measurement-foundation/) service.
