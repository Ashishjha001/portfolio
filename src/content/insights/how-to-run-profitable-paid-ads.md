"We ran Google Ads, it was expensive, we stopped." The Data Decision Consulting team hears this constantly, and the post-mortem almost always finds the same three gaps: no defined offer, no measurement path into the CRM, and no pre-agreed break-even line.

This is the implementation the Data Decision Consulting team runs inside [Paid Acquisition & Demand Generation](/contact/?service=paid-acquisition-performance#request-a-brief).

## Step 1: The math before the media

With finance, the Data Decision Consulting team computes the ceiling from the [break-even CAC worksheet](/insights/break-even-cac-worksheet/): contribution per customer, payback tolerance, close rate → maximum cost per qualified lead. Every channel target inherits from that line.

**Rule:** no campaign launches without a written cost-per-qualified-lead ceiling and a stopping rule.

## Step 2: The offer decides everything

B2B buyers do not click "Learn More". The Data Decision Consulting team designs one offer per audience stage:

- **Problem-aware:** a diagnostic, benchmark or template that proves expertise.
- **Solution-aware:** a scoped assessment or comparison guide.
- **Product-aware:** a demo or audit with a clear deliverable and timeline.

The offer must be something the sales team wants to receive. If sales would call it a junk lead, marketing should not be bidding for it.

## Step 3: Targeting and structure

The Data Decision Consulting team structures accounts so data accumulates cleanly:

- One campaign per audience-intent combination; tight ad groups.
- Exact and phrase match for intent terms; broad only with a guarded budget.
- LinkedIn for ICP filters (industry, role, company size); Google for active intent; retargeting as a layer, not a strategy.
- Exclusions from day one: existing customers, job seekers, competitors.

## Step 4: Landing pages that continue the promise

Ads never point at a homepage. Each campaign gets a page that repeats the offer, states the deliverable, shows proof, and asks for the minimum fields. The [conversion playbook](/insights/how-to-increase-conversion-rate/) defines the page rules the Data Decision Consulting team applies.

**Test:** the page loads fast, the headline matches the ad exactly, and the form works on a phone.

## Step 5: Measurement into the pipeline

Campaign URLs follow the [governance taxonomy](/insights/utm-governance-b2b/) so every click reaches the CRM as a named source. The Data Decision Consulting team reports:

| Metric                             | Source       | Cadence   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------ | --------- |
| Spend, clicks, CPL                 | Ad platforms | Weekly    |
| Qualified leads by campaign        | CRM          | Weekly    |
| Cost per qualified lead vs ceiling | CRM + spend  | Monthly   |
| Pipeline and revenue by source     | CRM          | Quarterly |

## Failure modes the Data Decision Consulting team designs against

- Optimizing to form fills that sales rejects → fix the offer definition.
- Scaling before the measurement path is proven → fix Step 5 first.
- Judging a campaign inside two weeks → pre-commit windows and stopping rules.
