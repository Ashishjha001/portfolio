  <p><strong>Route by readiness and fit, not by the form name.</strong> High-fit, high-intent buyers may benefit from immediate sales scheduling; uncertain requests need fast qualification and clear catch-all handling.</p>
</div>

Someone completes a high-intent demo form. Should they immediately book time with sales, or wait for an SDR to qualify them?

The wrong answer is one permanent rule for every lead.

Route by **readiness and fit**, not by the name of the form. Routing sits between form submission and meeting booking in the [Demand-to-Demo diagnostic framework](/insights/traffic-up-demo-bookings-down/).

## Send directly to sales when

- The company matches your target account criteria.
- The buyer has clear commercial intent.
- Deal size justifies specialist time.
- Territory and ownership are known.
- The required sales representative has relevant availability.
- The form already collected enough information to prepare a useful conversation.

For these leads, another qualification call can feel like asking the buyer to explain the same problem twice.

Chili Piper’s analysis of nearly four million mostly B2B form submissions found that 66.7% of qualified submissions booked a meeting when immediate scheduling was available. This is vendor-customer data, not a universal market benchmark, but it shows the size of the gap that manual follow-up can create.

## Route through an SDR when

- Fit is uncertain.
- The inquiry may be support, partnership, recruitment or education rather than purchase intent.
- The product has several sales motions and needs discovery before assignment.
- Enterprise requests require account research or coordination.
- Capacity is limited and poor-fit meetings regularly consume specialist time.

The SDR should add information or direction, not act as a waiting room.

## Use a three-path model

1. **High fit + high intent:** offer immediate scheduling with the right sales owner.
2. **Unclear fit or intent:** route to fast SDR qualification.
3. **Clear non-fit:** provide a useful alternative, such as documentation, partner information or a future-fit path.

Every routing system also needs a catch-all path. Otherwise, a technically valid lead that matches no rule can disappear silently.

Measure each path using:

- Time to assignment
- Qualified-to-booked rate
- Show-up rate
- Meeting-to-opportunity rate
- Pipeline created
- Disqualification reason

Fast routing is valuable only when it sends the buyer to the right conversation. The goal is not “every lead goes directly to an account executive.” The goal is:

> The right buyer can reach the right person without unnecessary delay.
