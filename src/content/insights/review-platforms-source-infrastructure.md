  <p><strong>Review platforms are frequently cited sources in buying answers, which makes them part of your source infrastructure.</strong> Treat them with the same care as your own domain: accurate listing, current facts, and a dated observation of what they say about you.</p>
</div>

Ask an assistant for category recommendations and the answer often cites comparison and review platforms. For buyers, those citations carry independence that your own pages cannot. For vendors, that makes the platforms infrastructure: they shape answers whether or not you participate.

## The framework

| Layer          | Question                                | Action                                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Presence       | Is the listing accurate and complete?   | Claim, correct fields, add facts                               |
| Recency        | Do recent, substantive reviews exist?   | A steady, honest review process                                |
| Representation | Does the description match positioning? | Correct stale category or pricing                              |
| Observation    | What do answers actually cite today?    | Dated [baseline runs](/insights/build-ai-visibility-baseline/) |
| Conduct        | Are solicitation rules followed?        | Follow each platform's policy                                  |

## What this framework is not

- It is not a claim about how any engine weighs review sites. Weighting is internal and unverifiable from outside.
- It is not an argument for review-gating or incentivised volume; platforms prohibit it and buyers detect it.
- It is not a one-time fix. Listings drift; facts change.

## A dated observation beats an assumption

Run the [visibility baseline method](/insights/build-ai-visibility-baseline/) over your category prompts and record which domains appear as cited sources. Keep the query frame fixed and the dates recorded, per the [methodology](/methodology/). Repeat the sample after meaningful changes.

The observation may show review platforms, your own domain, documentation sites or analyst pages. Whatever it shows, the next decision stands on a dated sample instead of a conference anecdote.

## Decision rules

- If a listing fact is wrong anywhere, correct it at the source platform, not via mass-update tools alone.
- If answers cite a comparison page with stale information about you, ask the platform for a correction with evidence.
- If you have no presence where answers cite heavily, decide deliberately: participate or accept the gap, in writing.
