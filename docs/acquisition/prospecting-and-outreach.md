# Prospecting and outreach playbook

## Account qualification

An account enters the list only when it meets the core ICP and has a reason to act now.

Required account fields:

- Company, domain and B2B SaaS category.
- Approximate stage or commercial maturity from a public source.
- Buyer role and public business contact channel.
- One observable trigger.
- One relevant website, search or measurement observation.
- Best-fit flagship service.
- Source URL and date checked.

Use public business information and permitted data sources. Do not scrape private profiles, guess personal emails or buy unverifiable bulk lists.

## Useful triggers

- A new head of growth, marketing or RevOps.
- A funding event or new-market expansion.
- Active hiring for SEO, content, analytics or demand generation.
- A site migration, rebrand or major product launch.
- Strong content activity with weak commercial paths.
- Visible indexation, architecture or conversion-path problems.
- Public discussion of attribution, lead-quality or CRM disagreement.

## Research note format

Write one sentence before contacting anyone:

> I noticed [specific public observation], which may make [commercial decision] harder because [short reason].

If the sentence could be sent to 100 companies unchanged, the research is not specific enough.

## Four-message sequence

### Email 1 — observation

Subject: question about {{company}}’s {{specific area}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed {{specific, verifiable observation}}.

That can make it difficult to tell {{relevant decision or consequence}}. I run Data Decision Consulting, a founder-led B2B SaaS SEO and measurement practice.

Would it be useful if I sent the two or three checks I would run first?

Ashish

### Email 2 — useful check

Hi {{first_name}},

One quick check related to my note: {{short, account-specific diagnostic idea}}.

It will not prove {{explicit limitation}}, but it should show whether {{decision signal}} is worth investigating.

Worth sending the short checklist?

Ashish

### Email 3 — relevant resource

Hi {{first_name}},

This may be useful even if the timing is not right: {{one genuinely relevant Data Decision Consulting article, tool or methodology link}}.

The important part is {{one-sentence takeaway connected to the account}}.

If this is owned by someone else, who is the right person to compare notes with?

Ashish

### Email 4 — close the loop

Hi {{first_name}},

I will close the loop after this. Should I leave this with you, contact someone else, or revisit it later?

Ashish

## Rules

- One low-friction question per email.
- No fake familiarity, urgency, praise, results or client proof.
- Do not attach files in the first email.
- Stop on a negative reply or opt-out.
- Keep a suppression list and do not re-add contacts accidentally.
- Measure positive replies and fit calls, not opens.
