# 10-client acquisition plan

Updated: 2026-09-02  
Owner: Founder  
Objective: win 10 good-fit clients without ranking guarantees, fake-local pages or unverified proof.

## The operating principle

Ten clients should not be treated as an SEO forecast. Search is a compounding channel; outbound and referrals create conversations sooner. The practical system uses three connected motions:

1. **Capture existing intent** through the three flagship service pages.
2. **Create targeted conversations** with a small, researched prospect list.
3. **Compound authority** through the existing insights, research and free tools.

The website is the evidence layer for all three motions.

## Offer focus

Lead with only two diagnostic entry points:

- **B2B SaaS SEO and AI-search diagnostic** — for teams with weak organic discovery, uncertain AI-answer visibility or content that does not create qualified demand.
- **GA4 and attribution diagnostic** — for teams whose analytics, ad platforms and CRM disagree about lead source, demo quality or pipeline.

Implementation may follow a diagnostic, but it should not be the first promise.

## Funnel model

Do not invent conversion benchmarks. Record the real numbers weekly:

`researched accounts → valid contacts → positive replies → fit calls → written scopes → clients`

To plan activity, enter three observed rates after the first month:

- Positive-reply rate = positive replies / delivered first emails.
- Scope rate = written scopes / fit calls.
- Close rate = clients / written scopes.

Required delivered contacts for the remaining client target:

`clients needed ÷ close rate ÷ scope rate ÷ positive-reply rate`

Use a range, not a single forecast. If any rate is unknown, collect data before increasing volume.

## First 30 days — establish signal

- Verify the contact form and analytics events in production.
- Build a list of 50–75 B2B SaaS accounts that match the ICP.
- Prioritize observable triggers: recent funding, active SEO hiring, a site migration, new market expansion, visible tracking problems, heavy content investment or a new growth leader.
- Send researched outreach in small batches; every message must contain one account-specific observation.
- Publish or refresh one decision-grade article each week and link it to the relevant flagship service page.
- Ask existing professional contacts for introductions to B2B SaaS founders, growth leaders and RevOps leads.
- Record every stage in one simple CRM or spreadsheet.

## Days 31–60 — improve the offer

- Review objections and lost-fit reasons from real conversations.
- Update service-page FAQs only when the objection appears repeatedly.
- Turn anonymized, permission-safe diagnostic patterns into educational content; never imply a client result without approval.
- Create one downloadable decision tool only if it supports a frequent sales question.
- Test a small high-intent Google Search campaign only after form and qualified-lead tracking work end to end.
- Keep India and US campaigns separate so budget, location and lead quality can be judged independently.

## Days 61–90 — scale what is verified

- Increase outreach volume only if data quality and reply quality hold.
- Move spend toward queries that produce qualified conversations, not form volume alone.
- Build one location-relevant page only if Search Console shows demand and the page can include genuine local evidence. Do not publish city-name swaps.
- Ask successful clients for a factual testimonial or case study only after a real outcome exists and permission is documented.
- Create a referral loop for clients and trusted partners.

## Weekly scorecard

| Area     | Record                                                                                 |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Website  | service-page visits, contact starts, successful briefs, qualified briefs               |
| Outbound | accounts researched, valid contacts, delivered emails, positive replies, fit calls     |
| Sales    | scopes sent, scopes accepted, reasons lost, average time to decision                   |
| Search   | non-brand impressions, qualified query themes, service-page clicks, assisted enquiries |
| Delivery | active engagements, available capacity, completion quality, referral opportunities     |

## Stop rules

- Stop an outreach segment after enough delivered messages to show consistently poor fit; diagnose the list and offer before writing more copy.
- Stop paid search if conversion tracking is uncertain or queries are informational and unqualified.
- Stop publishing pages that have no distinct intent, evidence or internal role.
- Do not expand to more cities or services until the two flagship offers create repeatable qualified conversations.
