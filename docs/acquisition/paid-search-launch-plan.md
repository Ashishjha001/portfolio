# Paid-search launch plan

Paid search is a controlled acquisition test, not a shortcut to the number-one organic position.

## Launch gates

Do not spend until all are true:

- The production contact form has been submitted successfully from an ad landing page.
- `lead_form_started` and `lead_form_submitted` are visible in the analytics debugger.
- A qualified lead can be marked in the CRM or lead sheet and connected to campaign parameters.
- The service page and contact page make the same offer.
- A monthly test budget, maximum acceptable qualified-lead cost and delivery capacity are written down.

## Campaign structure

Keep campaigns small and interpretable:

1. **Brand** — exact and phrase variants of Data Decision Consulting. Low budget; protects navigational demand.
2. **B2B SaaS SEO** — high-intent service terms leading to `/services/seo/`.
3. **AI SEO / AEO / GEO** — high-intent service terms leading to `/services/ai-search-visibility/`.
4. **GA4 / attribution consulting** — high-intent service terms leading to `/services/measurement-foundation/`.

Separate India and United States campaigns. Use location option **Presence: people in or regularly in the targeted locations**, not “interest in”. Do not claim a local office where none exists.

## Match and query control

- Start with exact and phrase match.
- Review search terms at least twice weekly during the test.
- Add negatives from observed irrelevant queries; do not apply a large generic list blindly.
- Common categories to investigate include jobs, salary, course, certification, free, definition, template and software-only searches. Some may be useful for content but not for a consulting campaign.
- Keep brand and non-brand budgets separate.

## Conversion hierarchy

Primary optimization event: qualified project brief or qualified fit call.

Diagnostic events:

- Service-page CTA click.
- Contact form started.
- Contact form validation error.
- Contact form successfully submitted.

Do not optimize long-term toward button clicks or raw forms. Import qualified-lead and accepted-scope outcomes when the CRM process is reliable.

## Decision cadence

Judge in this order:

1. Search-term relevance.
2. Landing-page engagement and form starts.
3. Successful briefs.
4. Qualified briefs and fit calls.
5. Written scopes and clients.

Do not declare a winning market, city, keyword or ad from a handful of clicks.
