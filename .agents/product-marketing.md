# Data Decision Consulting — product-marketing context

Version: 1.0  
Updated: 2026-09-02  
Status: Working source of truth; unknowns are marked rather than guessed.

## Positioning

Data Decision Consulting is a founder-led B2B SaaS growth and SEO consultancy. The firm helps teams improve organic and AI-search visibility and build a defensible measurement path from discovery to pipeline.

Primary category: B2B SaaS growth and SEO consultancy.

Flagship services:

1. B2B SaaS SEO and AI-search visibility.
2. GA4, attribution and measurement consulting.

Paid acquisition, conversion work and implementation are secondary, scoped services. They should support the two flagship offers rather than compete with them in the main positioning.

## Ideal customer profile

Primary ICP: seed to Series B B2B SaaS companies with an established website, a commercial team and enough live demand to diagnose.

Likely buyers:

- Founder or CEO at an earlier-stage company.
- Head of Growth or VP Marketing.
- Demand generation or performance marketing lead.
- RevOps, marketing operations or analytics lead.

Strong-fit situations:

- Organic traffic or rankings exist, but qualified pipeline is unclear.
- The company is not appearing consistently in relevant AI answers and does not know which sources shape those answers.
- GA4, ad platforms and the CRM disagree about lead source or revenue.
- The team is considering more SEO or paid spend but cannot identify the current constraint.
- The buyer wants a defined diagnostic and a written scope, not an open-ended retainer by default.

Poor-fit situations:

- The buyer expects guaranteed rankings, guaranteed AI citations or instant results.
- There is no usable website, offer or market to evaluate.
- The request depends on fabricated proof, fake reviews or misleading local-presence claims.
- The buyer wants high-volume execution without access, measurement or decision ownership.

## Buyer problems and desired outcomes

| Problem | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Search activity is disconnected from pipeline | Know which organic work is commercially useful and what to do next |
| AI-answer visibility is anecdotal | Establish a dated, repeatable observation baseline with sources and limits |
| GA4 and CRM disagree | Identify breaks in the lead-source path and define a reconciliation plan |
| Marketing investment is spread across too many tactics | Identify the current growth constraint and prioritize a small number of actions |
| Agencies make claims the buyer cannot verify | Receive a written method, evidence boundary and decision-ready recommendation |

## Differentiation

- Measurement-first: recommendations are tied to observable evidence and the decision they support.
- Explicit evidence boundaries: sampled AI observations are not presented as universal rankings.
- Founder-led accountability: Ashish Jha reviews enquiries and leads the work.
- Published method and research library: prospects can inspect how the firm thinks before contacting it.
- No ranking, citation or revenue guarantees.

## Approved proof

Public proof currently available:

- Published methodology.
- Public research, insights, playbooks and free tools.
- A visible work/evidence ledger.
- Founder identity and verified public profiles listed in the entity definition.

Do not claim client logos, testimonials, revenue gains, ranking gains, review scores or credentials unless verified evidence is added to the repository.

## Conversion

Primary conversion: submit a project brief through `/contact/`.

Primary CTA language:

- Request an SEO diagnostic.
- Request a growth and measurement review.
- Send a project brief.

The first response should establish fit, evidence required and the next decision. A written scope must state fee, inputs, timing and limitations before work starts.

## Commercial unknowns

The following are intentionally unresolved:

- Pricing and minimum engagement value.
- Current qualified-lead, proposal and close rates.
- Delivery capacity per month.
- Geographic concentration of existing clients.
- Verified client outcomes and testimonials.
- Target ad budget and acceptable customer-acquisition cost.

These must be supplied or measured before forecasting revenue or scaling paid acquisition.

## Messaging guardrails

- Use direct, human language and concrete buyer situations.
- Lead with B2B SaaS SEO/AI-search visibility and GA4/attribution.
- Describe capability and method; do not promise rank, citations, pipeline or market leadership.
- Use “remote” unless a real, verifiable local office or service history exists.
- Do not create city-swapped doorway pages.
- Keep the domain, URLs, email addresses and legal/entity facts unchanged.
